ABOUT

A summer of guided research.

PAIRS pairs students with mentors for a summer of AI/ML work. You pick the applied fields you care about, read the scientific literature around them, and turn that into an original machine-learning research project.

PHASES

From fundamentals to your own research.

00
PRE-SUMMER

Foundations

Hands-on ML fundamentals in Google Colab: regression on real housing data, k-Means clustering and PCA for high-dimensional data, plus DataCamp and StatQuest to build intuition.

01
PHASE 1 · EXPLORE

Fields & Specializations

Pick 2–3 applied fields and 2–3 ML specializations that interest you. Define them, find real-world examples, and brainstorm the intersections that could become your project.

02
RESEARCH

Literature & Annotation

Dig into scientific journals, annotate research papers related to your field and specialization, and map the landscape with citation-network tools like ResearchRabbit, SciSpace and NotebookLM.

03
BUILD

Your Project

Develop your own AI/ML project, from concept and data to model, impact and ethics, with mentor feedback in live sessions and one-on-ones.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need ML experience to join?+

No. The program starts with a pre-summer foundations assignment (regression, clustering, PCA in Colab) and resources like DataCamp and StatQuest, so everyone builds the same base before choosing specializations.

Can I work in a group?+

Yes. Projects can be individual or group-based. Groups generally submit one deliverable together unless an assignment says otherwise.

How do one-on-one meetings work?+

Sign up via the one-on-one form for a 7:30–8:20 PM slot on the day you want. Slots depend on demand, so a specific day isn't guaranteed.

What will I have at the end of the summer?+

An annotated literature base in your chosen field, experience with the modern research toolkit, and your own AI/ML research project shaped by weeks of mentor feedback.

Where do sessions happen?+

Everything runs online — live evening meetings plus Google Classroom for materials, assignments, recordings and announcements.
